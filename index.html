Nova Sentinel

When the night falls, hope rises.

Guardian of the city • Cosmic energy • Fearless protector

Origin Story

When a meteor shower crossed the sky above Meridian City, one strange star fell into the old observatory. Teen scientist Nova Reyes discovered a crystal glowing at its centre. Instead of burning her, the crystal answered the curiosity and courage she had carried all her life.

The crystal bonded with Nova and gave her the ability to gather energy from starlight. At first, the power was difficult to control. She trained at night, learning to shape bright shields and move faster than a falling star. Each mistake taught her patience and responsibility.

Now Nova protects Meridian City from dangerous experiments and cosmic threats. She believes being a hero is not about being fearless; it is about helping people even when you feel afraid.

Powers and Abilities

  • Creates protective starlight shields
  • Releases focused cosmic energy bursts
  • Can glide short distances on light trails
  • Detects unusual energy signatures
  • Has enhanced speed under the night sky

Known Weaknesses

  1. Her energy fades when she overuses her powers.
  2. Thick clouds and underground spaces limit her access to starlight.
  3. Protecting several people at once divides her focus.
